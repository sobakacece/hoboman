# GDD

**Name:** *The Amazing Hobo in a Shopping Cart with a Shotgun*  
**Genre:** Speedrun Top-Down Shooter (?)
**References:** *Hotline Miami* (core gameplay loop), *My Friend Pedro* (feelings and experience)  

**Narrative:**  
The player starts as a hobo stuck in a shopping cart. While attempting to escape, they accidentally spark a revolution, plunging the world into chaos. The most dangerous organizations of the modern-day are now out to stop them, and all you have to stop them is a shotgun.

**Gameplay Summary:**  
Being stuck in a shopping cart limits your mobility - you move very slowly. Use the shotgun's recoil to optimaly move yourself across the level. Kill everybody and everyone along your way.

### Gameplay Loop  
1. Kill all enemies in the level.  
2. Reach the finish line.  
3. Achieve the best possible time (score).  
4. Progress to the next level.  

### Prototype Features  

1. **Recoil-Based Movement**  
   Shooting is your primary method of mobility. While you can still move with WASD, it's much slower. Mastering recoil, explosions, and environmental interactions is key to moving faster and achieving better times.  

2. **Ranking System**  
   Implement a basic ranking system with grades (like E to S) based on clear time only.

3. **Environment Interaction**  
   Barrels, weapons, etc.  

4. **Enemies**  
   Design simple enemies with basic pathfinding. They should actively seek out the player and drop weapons upon death, which the player can pick up.
